# Bujumbura Bitcoin Builders

PROJECT PROPOSAL: ORGANIZATION OF TWO 

HACKATHONS

Implemented by: Free Tech Institute

Partner: Vexl Company

Location: Bujumbura, Burundi

1. Background and Objective

As part of the Peer-to-Peer Bitcoin Educational Program, Free Tech Institute will organize two 

hackathons in Bujumbura, bringing together 30 Bitcoin enthusiasts and developers from 

Burundi.

These hackathons will be preceded by three theoretical and practical workshops focused on the 

following key themes:

 Understanding Bitcoin: How to conduct permissionless, peer-to-peer exchanges without 

intermediaries

 Introduction to applications such as Vexl and the importance of security in decentralized 

systems

 Practical insights on peer-to-peer Bitcoin trading without KYC

The practical sessions will emphasize:

 Learning Bitcoin fundamentals from scratch

 Running and interacting with a personal Bitcoin Core node

 Developing and testing Lightning Network applications in real time

 Hands-on sessions using multiple Lightning SDKs

 Contributing to open-source Bitcoin projects

At the end of the last hackathon, participants will present their solutions through pitch 

sessions, with selected projects receiving awards.

The hackathons aim to transform theoretical knowledge into practical solutions, with a strong 

focus on: “How to conduct permissionless, peer-to-peer exchanges without intermediaries.”

Key Objectives

 Promote the practical use of Bitcoin in a decentralized environment

 Encourage the development of local peer-to-peer exchange solutions

Foster innovation among young talents

 Identify and support high-impact initiatives

3. Hackathon Format

Each hackathon will be conducted over one full day and will include:

 Team formation

 Mentorship and solution development sessions

 Design of practical solutions addressing real-world challenges

 Final pitch presentations before a jury

 Best Pitch: USDT100

 Best Practical Solution (aligned with peer-to-peer exchange principles): USDT50

6. Expected Outcomes

 2 functional Bitcoin P2P prototypes developed by the end of the hackathons.

 30 participants trained and certified in Bitcoin, Lightning Network, and P2P trading, with 80% 
demonstrating practical competency.

 70% of participants successfully interact with a Bitcoin Core node and test at least one 
Lightning application.

 2 high-potential projects selected for mentorship and post-hackathon technical support.

 Marketing conducted before, during, and after the hackathons, generating at least 5 digital 
publications on BTC Shule and partner platforms.

 At least 10 peer-to-peer Bitcoin transactions completed by participants during the events.
